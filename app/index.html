      Roadmap to the platinum

      Built from the official descriptions alone: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Five stages, in order — finish one before the next where the stage says so. Each stage shows how far you are through it.

        Chapter trophies (Chapters 1–4 and the final mission) cannot be missed: finishing the story earns them. The 7 missable trophies are the ones tied to a single moment — see the Missables tab.

        Missable trophies — 7 to watch

        A trophy is flagged missable here when its official description ties it to a single moment that may not repeat — a partner moment, a one-spot stunt, a single retry, or a strange one-off event. If you pass that moment without it, replay the mission or start a new game. Everything else is repeatable or awarded for finishing the story. These flags are inferred from the official descriptions, not confirmed in-game.
